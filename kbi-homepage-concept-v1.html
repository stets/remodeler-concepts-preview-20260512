Concept focus

The direction is not to make the brand flashy. It is to make the existing strengths easier to see: specialization, finished work, and a clear way to start.

1

Trust near the top

Surface verified longevity, showroom details, service area, and proof points where homeowners can see them immediately.

2

One clear primary action

Use “Schedule a consultation” or “Start a design conversation” consistently instead of scattering generic contact language.

3

Portfolio with context

Pair photos with project type, materials, room goals, or before/after notes so the gallery sells capability, not just aesthetics.
